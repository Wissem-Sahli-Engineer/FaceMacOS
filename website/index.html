How it works

Four steps.
Zero effort.

01

Close the lid

Walk away. FaceMacOS keeps running quietly in your menu bar, even after a restart.

02

Open it

The moment the screen wakes, scanning starts. The Face ID animation appears in the notch — right on the lock screen.

03

Look & blink

Your face is matched on-device, and a blink proves you're really there — a photo won't do.

04

You're in

FaceMacOS types your password for you. Lid open to desktop in about two seconds.

Apple put Face ID in your iPhone. We put it in the Mac you already own — using the camera it already has, running entirely on the chip it already has.

Features

More than a lock screen.

Automatic unlock

Scanning starts as soon as you open the lid or wake the screen. No clicks, no keys.

Notch animations

Scanning, success and failure animations live in the notch — visible even above the lock screen.

Face vault

A private note that opens only for your face. Press ⌥⌘F, look, blink.

Gesture commands

Blink twice to open an app. Look left to run a Shortcut. Your face becomes a keyboard.

Your color

Pick any accent color for the animations and the interface. Make it feel like yours.

Private by design

No cloud, no account, no photos saved. Face data and password stay in your Keychain.

Personalize

Make it yours.

Choose the color of every scan, ring and checkmark. Try it — the whole page follows.
