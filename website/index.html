How it works

Four steps.
Zero effort.

01

Close the lid

Walk away. FaceMacOS keeps running quietly in your menu bar, even after a restart.

02

Open it

The moment the screen wakes, scanning starts. The Face ID animation appears in the notch — right on the lock screen.

03

Look & blink

Your face is matched on-device, and a blink proves you're really there — a photo won't do.

04

You're in

FaceMacOS types your password for you, and the lock screen slides away.

Apple put Face ID in your iPhone. We put it in the Mac you already own — using the camera it already has, running entirely on the chip it already has.

Features

More than a lock screen.

  1. Scanning starts the moment the screen wakes. No clicks, no keys — lid open to desktop in a few seconds.

  2. Scanning, blink and success animations play right in the notch — even above the lock screen.

  3. A private note that stays encrypted in your Keychain and opens only for your face. Press ⌥⌘F, look, blink.

  4. Blink twice to open an app. Look left to run a Shortcut. Up to six gestures, each with its own action.

  5. Starts when you log in, stays in the menu bar, and restarts itself if it ever stops.

  6. No cloud, no account, no photos saved. Face data and your password never leave your Mac's Keychain.
